**Pasta salad**, known in Italian as **_insalata di pasta_** or **_pasta fredda_**, is a dish prepared with one or more types of [pasta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasta "Pasta"), almost always chilled or [room temperature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Room_temperature "Room temperature"), and most often tossed in a [vinegar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vinegar "Vinegar"), [oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooking_oil "Cooking oil") or [mayonnaise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayonnaise "Mayonnaise")-based dressing. It is typically served as an [appetiser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hors_d%27oeuvre "Hors d'oeuvre") ([antipasto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antipasto "Antipasto")) or first course (_[primo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian\_meal\_structure#Formal\_meal\_structure "Italian meal structure")_).

## Origin

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Pasta_salad&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Origin")]

Pasta salad appears in several food traditions. Claudia Roden describes cold pasta dishes prepared by Italian and Jewish communities for the Sabbath.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasta_salad#cite_note-1) American versions grew in the mid twentieth century through Italian immigrant cooking.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasta_salad#cite_note-2) Cold macaroni salads appeared in early twentieth century American cookbooks. Recipes identified as pasta salad appeared in print by the early 1960s.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasta_salad#cite_note-3) Italian antipasto traditions and Sicilian pasta fredda also shaped the idea of a chilled pasta dish.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasta_salad#cite_note-4)

## Ingredients

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Pasta_salad&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Ingredients")]

Pasta salad uses short types of pasta such as [rotini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotini "Rotini"), [farfalle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farfalle "Farfalle"), [penne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penne "Penne"), or [macaroni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macaroni "Macaroni"). Recipes often include vegetables, cheeses, or herbs.[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasta_salad#cite_note-5) Dressings use vinaigrette or mayonnaise in different regions.[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasta_salad#cite_note-6) The dish is usually served cold or at room temperature.[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasta_salad#cite_note-7)
